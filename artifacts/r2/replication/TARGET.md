## Main claim (C1)

Attention weights are frequently weakly correlated with gradient-based feature
importance; very different attention distributions can yield equivalent
predictions → standard attention modules should not be treated as explanations.

## Secondary claim (C2)

Pattern holds across multiple datasets/encoders/attention types in the paper.
